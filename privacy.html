Privacy policy

This page explains what ARFINITY Studio (“we”, “us”) does with the details you send through the “Get 3 creative worlds” form on arfinity.studio. It is a plain-language summary. If you have questions, contact support@arfinity.studio.

What we collect

When you submit the form we collect: your brand or business name, your website or Instagram address, your business category, your name, your business email address, your WhatsApp number, the creative world you are most interested in, an optional description of the product or campaign, and whether you agreed to the two consent options below.

We do not use cookies for this form, and we do not collect your IP address in our database. Hosting providers may keep standard server logs for security purposes.

Why we use it

  • To respond to your request. We use your details to prepare and send you three creative directions for your brand and to contact you personally. This is the only purpose of the required consent.
  • Marketing (optional). If you tick the optional box, we may send you occasional updates and examples of our work by email. You can leave it unticked. Ticking it does not affect your request, and we keep it separate from the request consent.

Where it is stored and who sees it

Submissions are stored in a secure database hosted by Supabase. Access is restricted to the ARFINITY team. The public website cannot read stored submissions. Each new submission is also sent by email to the ARFINITY team through Resend, our email delivery provider. We do not sell your details.

How long we keep it

We keep your request while we work on it and for up to 24 months after our last contact, unless you ask us to delete it sooner. If you did not tick the marketing box, we do not send you marketing messages.

Your rights

You can ask us to show, correct, export, or delete the details you have sent, and you can withdraw marketing consent at any time. Email support@arfinity.studio from the address you used on the form, and we will respond.

Children

The form is for brands and businesses. Do not submit personal details on behalf of a child.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top of the page shows the latest version.
